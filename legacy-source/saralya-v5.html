Straight answers

Questions we hear every week.

Is Saralya an RBI-regulated entity?
No. Saralya is a technology infrastructure provider. Lending is conducted by our customer institutions under their own licences.
Do we have to rip out FinnOne or our CBS?
No. Saralya plugs in via REST APIs and webhooks. Most deployments keep the LOS and CBS entirely untouched.
Where does customer data live?
Entirely inside India — AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1). Workspace-isolated per customer. AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, DPDP-aligned with row-level erasure.
How is this different from a general-purpose LLM?
Trained on anonymised Indian banking data and tuned on RBI Master Directions. It reasons in bureau tradelines and GST filing cadence, not generic web text.
Who owns the model outputs and audit trail?
You do. Every decision, score and reason code is yours — exportable and portable. If you leave, the audit history goes with you.
